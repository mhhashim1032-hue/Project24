About

Practical QA for website changes.

SiteShiftKit helps website owners, developers and technical SEO practitioners catch avoidable migration problems with simple browser-based tools and field guides.

Our focus

We focus on website migration quality assurance: comparing URL inventories, reviewing redirect mappings, inspecting robots.txt, parsing XML sitemaps, checking canonical declarations and preparing useful content indexes.

How the tools work

Current tools process the data you paste in your browser. They do not send those text inputs to a SiteShiftKit application backend. They are intentionally lightweight and do not currently crawl arbitrary domains or verify live server responses.

Editorial approach

Guides aim to explain practical workflows and limitations. We do not promise search rankings, indexing, traffic, AI citations or revenue outcomes.

Independence

SiteShiftKit is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google or any search engine provider.

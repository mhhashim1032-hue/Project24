AI search readiness: a practical website checklist

Practical content, crawlability, structured data and measurement practices for websites seeking visibility in AI-assisted search.

Make the source useful and accessible

Publish original information that answers real questions. Use descriptive page titles, clear headings, concise definitions, examples, supporting evidence and useful internal links.

Keep important facts crawlable

Do not hide the only copy of essential content behind inaccessible scripts, images or gated interactions. Provide meaningful HTML text and ensure pages are not unintentionally blocked.

Use structured data accurately

Choose schema types that match the visible page. Keep organization, author, product and article information consistent. Structured data can help systems interpret content, but does not guarantee rich results or AI citations.

Build trust

Identify the organization and authors, explain editorial standards, cite primary sources, correct outdated pages and make contact information easy to find.

Measure what matters

Track qualified organic traffic, conversions, referral sources and branded demand. AI search visibility tools and citation reports can be incomplete; avoid treating a single score as a business outcome.

No file, markup or optimization can guarantee inclusion in AI answers or higher rankings.

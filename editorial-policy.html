Editorial Policy

How SiteShiftKit creates, reviews and updates practical website migration and technical SEO guides.

Purpose

Our guides aim to help website owners and developers perform safer website migrations and understand technical SEO workflows. We prioritize practical steps, accurate limitations and clear explanations over exaggerated promises.

Research and sources

When a guide discusses search engine behavior, we aim to use primary documentation from search engine providers and standards organizations. We distinguish official guidance from common industry practice and from our own recommendations.

Accuracy and corrections

Technical details can change. We review content when relevant standards or product behavior changes and welcome corrections through our issue tracker.

Originality

We aim to publish useful, original explanations and examples. We do not intentionally copy other publishers' articles or publish pages solely to target keyword variations.

Independence

SiteShiftKit is independent and is not affiliated with Google or other search engine providers. We do not guarantee rankings, indexing, traffic, AI citations or revenue.

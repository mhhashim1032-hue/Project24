Field notes

Practical migration guides.

Actionable explanations for developers, SEO teams and site owners.

CHECKLIST · 8 MIN

Website migration checklist: checks before launch

Inventory important URLs, map one-to-one redirects, preserve metadata, review canonical tags, test robots rules, validate sitemaps, annotate analytics changes and monitor server responses after launch. Keep a baseline crawl and compare it with the new site before declaring the migration complete.

Before launch: crawl old URLs, export analytics landing pages, identify backlinks and create redirect mappings. At launch: test representative redirects, remove staging blocks and submit the new sitemap. After launch: monitor 404s, redirect hops, index coverage and organic landing-page changes.

TECHNICAL SEO · 6 MIN

Build a redirect map without redirect chains

Map each retired URL directly to its closest relevant final destination. Avoid sending old URL A to B when B immediately redirects to C. Normalize trailing slashes and hostnames, preserve query parameters where appropriate, and test final response codes on the deployed server. Do not redirect every deleted page to the homepage; a genuine 404 or 410 may be more accurate.

CRAWLABILITY · 5 MIN

Robots.txt during a redesign

Staging environments often use broad disallow rules. Verify temporary restrictions do not reach production. Robots.txt controls crawling, not guaranteed deindexing; a blocked URL may still be known through links. Review every user-agent group, disallow path and sitemap declaration.

AI SEARCH · 6 MIN

AI search readiness: practical checks, not promises

Make important information accessible, well structured and easy to cite. Use descriptive headings, accurate metadata, internal links, clear author and organization information, and valid structured data that matches visible page content. Keep key facts in crawlable HTML. Measure referrals and citations rather than assuming a file or markup creates AI visibility.

About SiteShiftKit

Independent tools for better website operations.

SiteShiftKit is an independent collection of browser-based utilities and educational material for website owners, developers and technical SEO practitioners. Its initial focus is website migration quality assurance: comparing URL inventories, reviewing redirect mappings and inspecting common technical files.

Privacy and limitations

Tool inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not intentionally transmitted to a SiteShiftKit application server. Hosting providers may process ordinary request metadata. Do not paste passwords, private keys or confidential customer data. These tools do not currently crawl arbitrary websites, fetch remote URLs or verify live HTTP status codes. Treat results as preliminary checks and confirm them against your production environment.

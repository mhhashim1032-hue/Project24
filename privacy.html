Privacy policy

Tool inputs

SiteShiftKit's current utilities run in your browser. Text pasted into the tools is processed locally by the page and is not intentionally transmitted to a SiteShiftKit application server. Avoid entering passwords, private keys, personal data or confidential client information.

The hosting and delivery provider may process technical request information such as IP address, user agent, request path, timestamps and security events as part of delivering and protecting the website. Provider practices are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated and any required consent controls implemented before activation.

External links

Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are controlled by those websites.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website changes. For privacy questions, use the contact details on the Contact page.
